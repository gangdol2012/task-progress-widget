Claude Progress is a small floating window that shows what each Claude session is working on: its title, a bar that fills as Claude works through its task list, and a rough estimate of the time left. A session that needs you turns amber.

This installs:

  • the Claude Progress app, in Applications;
  • a Claude mod and terminal hooks in ~/.claude, turned on in Claude's settings.json (your other settings are kept, and a backup is saved first);
  • a login item, so the widget opens when you log in.

Works with the Claude desktop app (Code tab) and Claude Code in a terminal.
